An environment is everything around us, including all living and non-living things that interact to sustain life on Earth.Key


Components of the EnvironmentBiotic components:
All living things like plants, animals, birds, and microorganisms. Abiotic components: Non-living elements such as air, water, soil, sunlight, and temperature.

Earth's four major spheres:
Atmosphere:
The layer of gases surrounding the planet.

Hydrosphere:
All the water on Earth.

Lithosphere (or Geosphere):
The solid rock and soil outer shell.

Biosphere:
The global zone where life exists.

Major Environmental ChallengesClimate change: Driven by greenhouse gas emissions and deforestation.
Pollution: Contamination of air, water, and soil with chemicals and plastics.
Biodiversity loss: The rapid decline and extinction of plant and animal species.Ways to Protect the Environment The Three Rs: Reduce consumption, reuse items, and recycle materials.Renewable energy: Switch to clean power sources like solar and wind energy.
Conservation: Protect natural habitats and plant new trees to maintain sustainability.You can check updates and facts on global conservation efforts via the UN Environment Programme.
