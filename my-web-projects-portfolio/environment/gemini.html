An environment is everything that surrounds us. It includes all living things like plants and animals, and non-living things like air, water, and soil.

Understanding Ecosystems

The environment includes all living and non-living things that interact continuously to sustain life on Earth.

Key Components of the Environment

🐾 Biotic Components

All living entities in an ecosystem that interact with one another.

Plants Animals Birds Microorganisms

☀️ Abiotic Components

Non-living physical and chemical elements in the environment.

Air Water Soil Sunlight Temperature

Earth's Four Major Spheres

💨 Atmosphere

The protective layer of gases surrounding the planet.

💧 Hydrosphere

Contains all the water found on, under, and over Earth.

🪨 Lithosphere

The solid rock, soil, and outer shell of the Earth.

🌍 Biosphere

The global zone encompassing all living organisms.

⚠️ Major Environmental Challenges

  • Climate Change: Driven by greenhouse gas emissions and deforestation.
  • Pollution: Contamination of air, water, and soil with chemicals and plastics.
  • Biodiversity Loss: Rapid decline and extinction of plant and animal species.

🛡️ Ways to Protect the Environment

  • The Three Rs: Reduce consumption, reuse items, and recycle materials.
  • Renewable Energy: Switch to clean power sources like solar and wind energy.
  • Conservation: Protect natural habitats and plant new trees for sustainability.

You can check updates and facts on global conservation efforts via the UN Environment Programme (UNEP).
