SHAHI PANEER

🍽️ Shahi Paneer Recipe - Introduction

Hello friends! Welcome to my kitchen!

Today, we are going to make a delicious and popular Indian dish called Shahi Paneer. It is a rich, creamy, and flavorful curry made with paneer, tomatoes, cashews, and special Indian spices.

Shahi Paneer tastes amazing when served with roti, naan, paratha, or jeera rice.

So, let's get started and learn how to make this delicious Shahi Paneer recipe at home!

  • onion

Instructions

  1. Prepare the ingredients: Cut 250g paneer into cubes. Chop 2 onions, 2 tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and green chillies.
  2. Cook the gravy base: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan. Add onions, ginger, garlic, green chillies, and 12–15 cashew nuts. Cook until the onions become soft.
  3. Add tomatoes and spices: Add chopped tomatoes, ¼ tsp turmeric powder, ½ tsp red chilli powder, and salt. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  4. Blend the mixture: Allow it to cool slightly, then blend it into a smooth paste.
  5. Prepare the curry: Heat 2 tbsp butter in a pan. Add 1 bay leaf, 2 green cardamoms, a small cinnamon stick, 2–3 cloves, and ½ tsp cumin seeds. Sauté briefly.
