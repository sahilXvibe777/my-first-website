WHAT IS AI (ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE)

Artificial intelligence: (AI) is a set of technologies that allows computers and machines to do tasks that normally need human thinking. These tasks include learning, solving problems, understanding language, and making decisions.

HOW AI IS WORK?

a)Data input: Computers are fed large amounts of information. b)Pattern spotting: The system looks for patterns and rules in the data. c)Predictions: The system uses these patterns to make choices or guess what comes next.
