Our Environment

An environment is everything around us, including all living and non-living things that interact to sustain life on Earth.

Start Learning ↓ How to Help

Components of the Environment

The environment is made up of living and non-living elements that interact with one another.

🌿

Biotic Components

All living things like plants, animals, birds, and microorganisms.

💧

Abiotic Components

Non-living elements such as air, water, soil, sunlight, and temperature.

Earth's Four Major Spheres

Four connected systems help make Earth a place where life can exist.

☁️

Atmosphere

The layer of gases surrounding the planet.

🌊

Hydrosphere

All the water on Earth.

🪨

Lithosphere

The solid rock and soil outer shell.

🦋

Biosphere

The global zone where life exists.

Major Environmental Challenges

Understanding major problems is the first step toward protecting our planet.

01

Climate Change

Driven by greenhouse gas emissions and deforestation.

02

Pollution

Contamination of air, water, and soil with chemicals and plastics.

03

Biodiversity Loss

The rapid decline and extinction of plant and animal species.

Ways to Protect the Environment

Small actions can contribute to a healthier and more sustainable environment.

♻️ The Three Rs

Reduce consumption, reuse items, and recycle materials.

☀️ Renewable Energy

Switch to clean power sources like solar and wind energy.

🌳 Conservation

Protect natural habitats and plant new trees to maintain sustainability.

🌎 Stay Informed

Learn about global conservation efforts and environmental facts.
